Resources & technology

A refrigerated fleet built for Europe

Our vehicles are equipped to keep the cold chain intact on any European route. Traceability and temperature control technology come as standard, not as an extra.

Equipment

Latest-generation refrigerated trailers

Euro 6 tractor units pulling ATP-certified refrigerated semi-trailers with high-performance cooling units.

Standard frigo trailer

33 Euro pallets capacity, −25 °C to +25 °C range, homogeneous ventilation, channelled floor.

Bi-temperature

Movable bulkhead to carry two products with different temperature requirements in the same trip.

GPS & telematics

Real-time position, route history, geofencing alerts and shared access on request.

Temperature probes

Continuous recording with dual calibrated probes. Full history delivered as a PDF after each trip.

Euro 6 engines

All tractor units comply with the latest European emissions standard, reducing the service's carbon footprint.

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled servicing at official workshops. Cooling units with calibration and fast-response contracts.
