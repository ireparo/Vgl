Specialisation

Industries we serve

Cold chain is not the same for every product. Each sector has its own temperature, timing, documentation and handling needs. At VG Logistics we focus on the ones we know inside out.

Meat

Fresh, chilled and frozen meat

We work with producers, slaughterhouses, cutting plants and meat distributors. Temperature-controlled transport from +1/+4 °C to −18/−25 °C, fully compliant with European sanitary and animal welfare regulations.

  • Fresh beef, pork, lamb and poultry
  • Frozen and deep-frozen product
  • By-products and ABP categories
  • Full temperature logging
🥩
🍎
Fresh produce

Fruit and vegetables

Lleida and the Mediterranean arc are among our core sourcing areas. We coordinate field pickups, packing stations and exports to the main European markets within the tight timings fresh produce demands.

  • Stone and pome fruit
  • Citrus, tropical fruit and berries
  • Leafy greens and vegetables
  • Tailored temperatures per product
Dairy

Milk and dairy products

The dairy sector demands an uninterrupted, traceable cold chain. We handle transport between dairies, processing plants and distribution, keeping the required temperature at every step.

  • UHT and pasteurised milk
  • Yogurts, desserts and fermented products
  • Cheese and cured products
  • Continuous temperature logging
🥛
Bakery

Bakery and pastry products

We transport industrial and artisan bakery, refrigerated and frozen dough, and pastry products that require temperature control from the factory to the distribution centre.

  • Industrial and artisan bakery
  • Refrigerated and frozen dough
  • Pre-baked pastry products
  • Cold chain preserved on every route
