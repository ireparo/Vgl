Operating in 12+ countries every week

From our hub in Lleida we connect the Iberian Peninsula with Europe's main logistics centres, with a special focus on Eastern corridors, where we deliver the operational continuity others don't.

Strategic hub in Lleida

Located in the Neoparc industrial estate, 8 minutes from both the A-2 motorway and the AP-2 toll road, we sit in the Ebro corridor with direct access to Barcelona, Zaragoza, Madrid and the French border. An ideal base to serve exporters across Iberia and importers receiving goods from Central and Eastern Europe.

20+ active destinations with regular weekly routes and quick-response capability for urgent shipments.
