What we do

International refrigerated transport services

We design the solution that best fits your product, temperature and delivery timing. From door-to-door full truckloads to customs and paperwork management, everything handled under a single point of contact.

🚛

Full truckload (FTL)

Exclusive refrigerated semi-trailer for your cargo. Door-to-door pickup and delivery across Europe with competitive transit times and full traceability.

  • Up to 33 Euro pallets per shipment
  • Temperature range from −25 °C to +25 °C
  • Bi-temperature available on request
📦

Refrigerated groupage (LTL)

Consolidated partial shipments with the same strict cold chain. Perfect for smaller volumes that need frequency and flexibility.

  • Regular pickups from Lleida
  • Product compatibility guaranteed
  • Dedicated documentation per shipment
🌡️

Temperature control

Continuous temperature monitoring with calibrated probes and automatic alerts on deviations. Full historical records delivered after every load.

  • Double control probes
  • PDF report per load
  • Valid ATP certification
🇪🇺

Specialised routes

Beyond the main European corridors, we run regular operations in destinations with more complex administrative and logistical challenges.

  • Romania, Hungary, Czechia
  • Poland, Slovakia, Slovenia
  • Local documentation handled
📑

Customs & documents

We take care of all the paperwork related to international food transport, including T1 transits and specific health requirements.

  • Digital and signed CMR
  • T1 customs transits
  • Health document control
🛰️

24/7 GPS tracking

Every vehicle in our fleet reports position and temperature in real time. We share access with customers on request.

  • Web tracking access
  • Automatic email alerts
  • Digital POD on delivery
