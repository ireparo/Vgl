Grow with us as a self-employed carrier

VG Logistics is both an operator and a shipper. That means we can offer self-employed carriers access to steady volumes, recurring routes and all the operational backup needed to professionalise your business.

📈

Steady volume

🗺️

European routes

Recurring work on the main corridors with optimised return legs.

⛽

Group supplies

On-time payments

Digital tools

Human treatment

Requirements

What we look for in a carrier

Transport licenceValid international freight authorisation.
VehicleEuro 5 or higher tractor unit, ideally Euro 6.
TrailerRefrigerated unit with valid ATP certification.
PaperworkCMR insurance, MOT, digital tachograph, ADR if required.
DriverCE licence, valid CPC and international experience.
AttitudeCommitment, punctuality and care for product and customer.
