Who we are

A family company with a European mindset

Vidal Gurghian Logistics S.L. was founded to solve a specific challenge: moving refrigerated goods across Europe with the rigour the cold chain demands and the close service of a team that knows every customer by name.

Our story

From an idea to a European reference operator

VG Logistics was born in Lleida with one clear goal: to offer solutions for every need in international refrigerated road transport. Combining deep knowledge of the food sector and hands-on operational experience on European routes, we built a company focused on detail and service.

Today, thanks to the trust of Spanish and international shippers, we operate every week in more than twelve countries and run regular routes on complex corridors such as Eastern Europe.
