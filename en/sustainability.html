Our commitment

Cleaner, more efficient transport

Moving food across Europe has an environmental footprint. Our role is to reduce it as much as possible without compromising the cold chain, while keeping an eye on where the sector is heading.

Our actions

Concrete measures in daily operations

🌱

Euro 6 fleet

All our tractor units comply with the strictest emissions standard in force in the European Union.

🗺️

Optimised routing

We minimise empty kilometres by combining loads and planning smart return legs.

❄️

Efficient cooling units

Low-consumption equipment with preventive maintenance to reduce leaks and waste.

📊

Carbon footprint

We report estimated emissions per shipment when the customer asks for it.

👷

Driver welfare

Routes compatible with rest periods and decent working conditions.

♻️

Paperless operations

Digital documentation (e-CMR, digital POD, PDF reports) whenever legally possible.

Looking ahead

Where we want to go

Long-distance transport decarbonisation is a real challenge. We are watching closely and ready to adopt viable solutions as they mature.

⚡

Alternative energy

We track the evolution of HVO, LNG, hydrogen and electric traction for long-haul.

🧊

Sustainable refrigeration

Lower-impact cooling units and low-GWP refrigerants.

🤝

Sector collaboration

We work with customers to jointly measure and reduce chain emissions.
